# Four Field sources connected to Heart

New copies installed in Field L3C4 Heart Quaternion Field, L3C5 Heart Quaternion Swell, L3C6 Heart Paper Fractal, L3C7 Heart Vector Lattice. Original clips and shader files remain intact. These shaders already declared useful inputs; their old Wire patches did not expose them to Resolume. New wrappers wire all 37 numeric inputs through the existing Heart engine. Shader bodies are unchanged; source identities and descriptive headers are new.

Validation: all four compiled/rendered locally. Live Heart discovered every control. All 74 manual targets across 37 controls passed native readback. One structural drive per clip varied in native readback and paired actual-output captures were reviewed with alpha composited on black. Swell, Paper and Vector show visible changing structure. Quaternion Field's original default was very dim; a live starting combination of Field Scale .55, Sample Distance .55, Brightness .75 and Mean Radius .5 produces a clear colorful field (field-start.png). These are current clip settings, not changed shader defaults or a verified Home preset.

This verifies control coverage and sampled output, not exhaustive useful ranges, phase-continuous rate modulation, Home/Settle, or GPU performance. Paper Fractal remains in Field, where full-frame geometry is appropriate; Figure containment rules do not apply to this placement.

Remaining original sources without exposed controls: Concentrip, TwoFiftySixBiatch, Kali ancient, and kali liberation of the true self. Their exact active resources and useful parameters need inspection before new copies are built.
