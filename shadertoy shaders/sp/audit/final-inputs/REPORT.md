# Final four input-free sources: control-ready copies

New copies are staged in Field: L3C9 Heart Concentrip (6 controls), L3C10 Heart Puls Lattice (7), L3C11 Heart Ancient Temple (16), L3C12 Heart Kali Liberation (10). Originals and their patch identities remain untouched. Kali Liberation is temporarily staged in Field: its original Figure placement does not satisfy containment across its camera/morph range, and these tests do not certify it for Figure.

Concentrip now exposes radial/angular density, blend threshold/width, phase and hue. Its copy also initializes the grayscale RGB branch, wraps HSV hue and guards zero divisors in its original modulo/density calculations. Puls exposes cell spacing, bar/bolt width, two octahedron expansion amounts and pulse/travel phase. Previously unconnected audio uniforms were replaced by explicit expansion controls. Its fisheye square root is guarded at wide aspect ratios. Existing automatic motion remains; phase controls offset it rather than multiplying the clock.

Ancient Temple and Kali Liberation already declared all their controls. Their shader bodies are byte-for-byte unchanged; only new headers/identities and complete Wire input wiring were added. Existing Heart native-source registration is used throughout.

Validation: all four compile and render locally. Heart discovers all 39 numeric controls. Each source passed two manual structural targets and a driven structural readback sample; paired actual Resolume output captures were visually reviewed with alpha composited on black. Concentrip ring density, Puls lattice, Temple morphology and Liberation form all visibly change. The initial all-control pass was 75/78 at a 220 ms readback interval: greenExpansion, fractalScale and saturation each missed one target. Repeating both targets on those controls at 800 ms passed 6/6. This does not distinguish delayed delivery from a dropped command; reliability remains unresolved. Raw evidence is in all-controls.json and retry-controls.json. These checks do not certify every parameter's visual usefulness, continuous smoothness, Home/Settle, containment or performance. Palette/iteration controls inherited from the Kali shaders can be discrete even though exposed as floats.

The formerly input-free source batch now has control-ready copies for all nine entries (Torus plus these four and the preceding four Field copies). Remaining work includes known dark/blank states, meaningful safe recovery, and final layer classification. Glyph remains deferred.
