**Status:** deferred until Heart works with mouse and keyboard. This checks hardware mappings onto the completed [Heart workflows](SHOW-CHECKLIST.md).

- [ ] **Receive:** operate intended Grid faders, knobs and buttons; identify their input messages.

- [ ] **PS4:** navigate, activate, go back and scroll through the working interface. Then repeat the complete playing phrase with Grid and PS4 together.

**Pass:** intended inputs control their assigned destinations through the existing armed output path. Selection changes leave fixed bindings unchanged.

**If blocked:** fix the input, binding or delivery failure, then repeat that check. No custom layout/Grab setup or comprehensive controller matrix is required here.
