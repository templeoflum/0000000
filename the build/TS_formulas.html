ℹ how it works — reference (stub)

Construction. Each cycle is [rotate, rotate, translate] applied to a seed point. The outward translation each cycle is d = k·(current radius). Because revolving a radius-r figure at distance kr produces a new radius (k+1)r, the displacement chain is d = k·(k+1)C−1·D (cycle C, seed radius D). This holds the central hole at a constant proportion (k−1)/(k+1) of the whole at every cycle. k=2 gives d=2r and hole = 1/3. With self-scaling off, a fixed-ratio rule is used instead (it collapses past level 4). The form grows ~(k+1)× per cycle, so the view is normalized to the bounding radius (= 1 unit).
