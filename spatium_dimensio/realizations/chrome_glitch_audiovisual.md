## Spec


**Overview:** The work as contemporary audiovisual performance. Synesthetic, futurist, the body as conduit for light. Used when the realization is intended for screen-native presentation — installation, music video, performance with projection.

**Voice preset:** Processed AV narrator. See Voice Reference. Override at Configuration: Layered (typical) or Solo (when foregrounding a single perspective). Affect: Numb (preset default, locked) — the flat, unmodulated affect is constitutive of the preset and of this realization's signature.

**Medium:** Atomic configuration — 3D render (Screen × Digital shader × Smooth rendered × Clean rendered × HDR/prismatic). No matching Tier 2 medium preset exists currently; the 3D digital render isn't catalogued as a preset bundle.

**Visual World preset:** Chrome-glitch. See Visual World Reference.

**Audio preset:** Stratified expansion. See Audio Aesthetic Reference.

**Math density:** High throughout — math primitives rendered as visible content of the work

**Movement affinities:** Strong IV, V, VII · Workable I, II · Resists III, VI

**Deployment notes:**
- The body's many openings of V render most natively in this realization
- III is hardest — chrome-glitch's unified chrome logic resists pluralism. Either lean into chromatic variants (different colors for different settlements) or substitute realization.
- VI similarly resists — digital cleanness reads cold for the gathering
- Cross-axis interaction: artist × realist × artistic peers is the working-note voice; deploy with confidence in shared aesthetic vocabulary
- Avoid drift toward generic sci-fi or vaporwave — preserve the specificity of the body as conduit

**Script preset:** AV script. See Script Reference.

**Presentation preset:** Glitched stream. See Presentation Reference.
