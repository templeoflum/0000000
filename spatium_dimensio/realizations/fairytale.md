# Fairytale

A realization of *Spatium Dimensio*. Currently a spec without generations — has not been constructed through the workflow.

> **Status:** Unverified. This realization was sketched as part of the framework's original catalog of realization patterns. It exists as a complete cross-layer spec but has not been used to produce tracked generations. When constructed end-to-end through the workflow, its compositional coherence will be verified.

## Spec


**Overview:** The work as story told to a child. Narrative warmth, embodied figures, math primitives folded into objects and gestures. Used when the realization is intended to reach without specialist vocabulary.

**Voice preset:** Storyteller. See Voice Reference. Override at Configuration sub-function: Character (narrator solo with embedded character voices for rod-maker, seeker, etc.) instead of preset's default Solo. Affect: Tender (override from preset default Composed) — the storyteller addresses the listener with care.

**Medium preset:** Watercolor. See Medium Reference.

**Visual World preset:** Storybook. See Visual World Reference.

**Audio preset:** Lecture register (drone + figure with acoustic breath-based read-aloud voice). See Audio Aesthetic Reference. Override at Phrasing: more flowing/narrative phrasing than the lecture register's symmetrical balance.

**Math density:** Low (primitives present as objects and gestures, not formulas)

**Movement affinities:** Strong I, II, V, VII · Workable III, VI · Resists IV

**Deployment notes:**
- Mythic narrator opening "there was a time when…" — establishes parallel construction
- Character voices bring registers the narrator wouldn't (engineer for rod-maker, child for the seeker)
- At IV, the realization struggles — fairytale's warmth resists the cage. Either lean into the cage as villain (which the work resists) or deploy a different realization for that movement.
- Avoid drift toward over-cute generic picture book — preserve the working-document quality of an actual handmade fairytale

**Script preset:** Telling script. See Script Reference.

**Presentation preset:** Illustrated chapter book. See Presentation Reference.
