## Spec


**Overview:** The work as ceremonial performance. Mythic register at congregational scale, gathered audience, ritual structure. Used when the realization is intended for shared meaning-making in a community context.

**Voice preset:** Mythic celebrant. See Voice Reference. Affect: Composed (preset default) — the celebrant's reverence is carried by Register × Stance × Positioning, not by affect; reverence emerges from the combination.

**Medium preset:** Oil/painted, Renaissance subset (Panel × Brush + Palette knife × Opaque layered × Sfumato or Hard inked × Opaque saturated). See Medium Reference.

**Visual World preset:** Mythic. See Visual World Reference.

**Audio preset:** Sacred drone. See Audio Aesthetic Reference.

**Math density:** Low to medium (primitives appear as sacred objects — gilded rods, painted axes, celestial spheres)

**Movement affinities:** Strong I, II, VI, VII · Workable III, V · Resists IV

**Deployment notes:**
- The work as liturgy: each movement is a section of a service
- Call-and-response peaks at VI (the gathered recognitions)
- At III, the chorus voices the many traditions in turn
- IV is hardest — ritual form resists the cage's compression. Deploy with deliberate handling or substitute realization for that movement.
- Avoid drift toward hollow ceremony — preserve specificity of what's being honored

**Script preset:** Liturgical script. See Script Reference.

**Presentation preset:** Liturgical scroll. See Presentation Reference.
