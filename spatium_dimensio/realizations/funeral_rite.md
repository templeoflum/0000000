# Funeral Rite

A realization of *Spatium Dimensio*. Currently a spec without generations — has not been constructed through the workflow.

> **Status:** Unverified. This realization was sketched as part of the framework's original catalog of realization patterns. It exists as a complete cross-layer spec but has not been used to produce tracked generations. When constructed end-to-end through the workflow, its compositional coherence will be verified.

---

## Spec


**Overview:** The work as a memorial service — a liturgical work in the mode of a funeral, wake, or memorial mass. For the death of the cathedral, the loss of the certainty that measurement was the world, the grief of unknowing what was once known. Sacred Ritual's structural cousin, but with the felt center turned toward elegy rather than reverent ceremony. Used when the realization needs to honor what is being released, when release is being received as loss before it can be received as freedom.

**Voice preset:** Mythic celebrant. See Voice Reference. Affect: **Grieving (override from preset default Composed)** — the elegiac mode requires the speaker's active grief; this is the realization's affective signature distinguishing it from Sacred Ritual. Other preset parameters (locked Register, Positioning, Configuration) accepted at their defaults.

**Medium preset:** Oil/painted, Renaissance subset. See Medium Reference. Same as Sacred Ritual.

**Visual World preset:** Mythic. See Visual World Reference. Two atomic overrides: Palette → *Earth and black* (instead of preset's default Earth and gold); Atmosphere → *Witnessed weight* (instead of preset's default Sacred/hieratic). Other sub-functions held at preset defaults: Light = Painted-from-within, Line = Classical contour, Figure = Classical idealized.

**Audio preset:** Sacred drone. See Audio Aesthetic Reference. Override at Timbre: add *Acoustic / struck* (tolling bronze bell) to the preset's bowed/breath-based palette. Override at Tonality: the just-intonation tonality of Sacred drone is held, with modal lament inflections appearing as ornament rather than as a separate tonality choice.

**Math density:** Low to medium (shared with Sacred Ritual). Math primitives appear as the same sacred objects, but rendered in the *Earth and black* palette — the gilded rods now in restrained gold against bone-white, the painted axes muted, the celestial spheres beside candles rather than catching painted light.

**Movement affinities:** Strong IV, V, VI, VII · Workable I, II · Resists III (the polyphony of settlements has a different felt weight here; handle as a procession of witnesses to lost certainty)

**Deployment notes:**
- The whole work as a memorial service, with the body of the deceased being "the certainty that measurement was the world"
- IV becomes the eulogy: the cathedral honored as it is set into the ground
- V becomes the slow recognition that what was lost was a smaller thing than what remains
- VI becomes the gathering of mourners across traditions, each in their own lament
- VII becomes the burial and the walking-on — the grief acknowledged, the field opened
- Drift to avoid: sentimentality — funeral rites at their best are clear-eyed, not lachrymose
- Drift to avoid: triumphalism about release — the work mourns first, then releases
- Demonstrates kit modularity through near-complete preset reuse with Sacred Ritual: same Voice preset, same Medium preset, same Visual World preset, same Audio preset, same Script preset. The differences are two atomic overrides on Visual World sub-functions, one atomic override on Audio timbre, and a different Presentation preset (Memorial scroll instead of Liturgical scroll). The two realizations are siblings, not duplicates — and the kit's tier discipline makes that visible.

**Script preset:** Liturgical script (reused from Sacred Ritual — demonstrates preset modularity). See Script Reference.

**Presentation preset:** Memorial scroll. See Presentation Reference.
