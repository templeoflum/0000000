## Spec


**Overview:** The work as popular-science talk. Scientist voice making the strange ordinary, the ordinary strange. Used when the realization is intended for an interested non-specialist audience — public talk, podcast, illustrated book.

**Voice preset:** Lecturer. See Voice Reference. Override at Positioning: General public (instead of preset's default Academic) to lower the gatekeeping. Affect: Composed (preset default), with the option to shift toward Curious at openings of new movements where the speaker invites the audience into a question.

**Medium:** Atomic configuration — hybrid; substrate could be Paper (illustrated book) or Screen (projected slides); mark-making and color behavior vary by chosen substrate. No single Tier 2 medium preset matches the realization's flexibility.

**Visual World:** Atomic configuration — hybrid of Geometric × Storybook. Restricted technical palette and precise technical line from Geometric (for math primitives); warm earth muted palette and gentle narrative atmosphere from Storybook (for figure and atmosphere). The realization deliberately composes across two presets.

**Audio preset:** Lecture register. See Audio Aesthetic Reference.

**Math density:** Medium (math primitives introduced clearly but not over-foregrounded)

**Movement affinities:** Strong I, II, III, V, VII · Workable IV, VI · Resists nothing

**Deployment notes:**
- The realization that meets the audience where they are and shows them the math without specialist gatekeeping
- At III, the lecture excels — enumeration of parallel coordinate systems is bread and butter
- At IV, scientist-realist-general-public produces "this is the model we use, here's what it does well, here's its scope" — the cage acknowledged without performing compression
- Avoid drift toward over-simplification — preserve scientist register's structural precision

**Script preset:** Lecture script. See Script Reference.

**Presentation preset:** Academic paper. See Presentation Reference.
