## Spec


**Overview:** The work as a naturalist's field notebook — a working scientist's record of an expedition that becomes, across seven entries, an account of one researcher's developing recognition that what they're measuring isn't what they thought it was. Used when the realization needs the felt weight of empirical patience and the slow shift of someone watching themselves change.

**Voice:** Atomic configuration — Scientist register × *Empirical* stance × Intimate positioning × Solo configuration × **Composed affect drifting toward Disoriented across the arc**. Doesn't compose to an existing Voice preset; closest is Intimate first-person but with Scientist register instead of Realist, and the new Empirical stance instead of Idealist-tempered-by-Empirical. The Empirical stance is the realization's distinguishing voice element — what makes this a working observer's record rather than a published account. The affect-shift across movements is also part of the realization's signature: the voice opens Composed (procedural, steady), develops Disoriented qualities by IV–V (the observer beginning to doubt their own observation), settles back toward Composed-but-altered by VII (the steadiness has changed).

**Medium:** Atomic configuration — Paper substrate × *Pencil / graphite* mark-making × *Lightly textured* surface × *Hand-drawn / variable* edges × Monochrome color (with occasional accent watercolor as composition element, not as preset choice). Doesn't compose to an existing Medium preset; uses three of the new Tier 1 options added for this realization.

**Visual World:** Atomic configuration — Naturalistic muted palette × Ambient illustrative light × Expressive loose line × Stylized narrative figure × Clear instructional atmosphere. Composes from existing Visual World sub-function options; doesn't fit any current Tier 2 preset cleanly. Reads as observational naturalist illustration without needing new vocabulary.

**Audio preset:** Field-recorded. See Audio Aesthetic Reference.

**Math density:** Low to medium. Math appears as the researcher's working sketches and measurements — small marginal diagrams, recorded values, attempts at formulae. Always provisional, frequently revised. The Pencil / graphite mark-making preserves the revisable quality at every layer.

**Movement affinities:** Strong I, II, V, VI · Workable III, VII · Resists IV (the notebook's intimacy and Empirical stance both resist architectural compression)

**Deployment notes:**
- Each movement is one entry, dated and located
- Drift to avoid: artificial polish — the notebook should feel actually used (smudges, crossings-out, second thoughts). The combination of *Pencil / graphite* mark-making and *Hand-drawn / variable* edges enforces this at the rendering level.
- The IV problem: handle by making it a single tense entry where the researcher catches themselves treating their model as the world; the Empirical stance's "I trust what I've directly observed" provides the lever for that recognition
- The arc of voice: opens credulous and procedural, develops uncertainty by IV, accommodates strangeness by V, settles into wonder by VII — the Empirical stance holds steady throughout while what's being observed widens
- If a second naturalist-empirical realization develops, the medium configuration here (Paper × Pencil/graphite × Lightly textured × Hand-drawn variable × Monochrome) is a candidate to promote to a Tier 2 *Sketchbook* medium preset
