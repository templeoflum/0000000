## Spec


**Overview:** The work as recovered testimony of someone whose own mind has become foreign to them. In the Ligotti / *True Detective* season-1 / Aickman-of-the-quiet-stories lineage — closer to philosophical horror than to cosmic horror. The wrongness is small, intimate, persistent: the speaker's own perception becoming an unreliable instrument; the world looking the same while everything has changed. Where Cosmic Horror's terror is scale, Existential Thriller's terror is intimacy — the dread that has no monster because the monster is the speaker's own dawning sense that they can no longer trust their footing. Used when the realization needs to render the recognition as ongoing wound rather than aftermath, and as interior rather than vast.

**Voice:** Atomic configuration — Everyman register × Empirical stance × Testimonial positioning × Solo configuration × Disoriented affect. Doesn't compose to an existing Voice preset; the combination is distinctive. The Disoriented affect is what distinguishes this from Cosmic Horror's Stricken — Stricken is the aftermath of having seen; Disoriented is the present-tense unraveling. The Everyman register (rather than Cosmic Horror's Engineer) gives the testimony its intimate-philosophical quality: the speaker has no professional vocabulary to wall off the recognition with. The Empirical stance and Testimonial positioning are shared with Cosmic Horror; the difference lives in affect and register.

**Medium preset:** Ink + wash. See Medium Reference. Shared with Cosmic Horror.

**Visual World:** Atomic configuration — Off-natural / sickly palette × Pall / wrong daylight × Painted realism / no visible line × Dislocated isolated figure × Quiet dread atmosphere. Differs from Cosmic Horror in two sub-functions: Off-natural / sickly palette (instead of Naturalistic muted — the colors themselves are slightly wrong, not just lit wrong) and Quiet dread atmosphere (instead of Cosmic indifference — the wrongness is intimate, present-tense, small-scale). The light and figure sub-functions match Cosmic Horror, giving the two realizations a visual family resemblance while the palette-and-atmosphere shifts establish the existential-thriller's distinct register.

**Audio:** Atomic configuration — Drone-based / unfixed tonality × Drone + figure texture × Electronic / synthesized + Acoustic / breath-based timbres × Arhythmic / event-based rhythm × Stillness / suspended pacing × Stereo / wide spatialization × Open / unphrased. Differs from Cosmic Horror in two sub-functions: Drone + figure texture (instead of Stratified / layered drones — a single rare event against the drone, more intimate than layered) and Stereo / wide spatialization (instead of Ambisonic / immersive — the listener placed close to the testifying voice rather than inside an unreliable space). The intimacy carries the existential frame; the audio is the speaker's voice in a quiet room where something is wrong, not the speaker placed inside the vastness.

**Math density:** Low to medium. Math appears less as evidence and more as ghost — the speaker's notations from before they understood, returned to with the new recognition. The Everyman register can't sustain heavy math; the math is fragmentary, sometimes the speaker's attempt to re-derive what they thought they knew.

**Movement affinities:** Strong IV, V, VI · Workable III, VII · Resists I, II (the early movements presume intact footing; the existential thriller's disorientation must have already begun)

**Deployment notes:**
- The whole work as recovered testimony from someone whose perception was the instrument that failed
- The Disoriented affect is constitutive and ongoing — not a state the speaker passes through to a resolution but the state from which the testimony is written
- IV is the recognition that the cathedral was the room the speaker had been thinking inside without knowing it; the dread is that the recognition itself may not be trustworthy
- V is the body as the site where the wrongness becomes inescapable — interior rather than the cosmic-horror version's wound-in-the-membrane
- VI is the fragmentary testimony of others, each disoriented in their own way; the gathering is not a fellowship but a constellation of unreliable narrators
- VII does not resolve: the writer sets down the rod or sets down the pen, but whether either gesture means anything is left to the reader
- Drift to avoid: melodramatic horror — the form is quiet, intimate, philosophical
- Drift to avoid: mystery-genre framing (the reader expecting solution) — the existential thriller offers no solution; the philosophical dread is the form
- Drift to avoid: cosmic-horror's vast-scale gestures — keep the scale small, the camera close, the voice present-tense
- Demonstrates the sibling pattern: shares three Tier 2 presets and most atomic choices with Cosmic Horror; the differences (palette, atmosphere, audio texture and spatialization, voice register and affect) establish the distinct register without requiring entirely separate vocabulary
