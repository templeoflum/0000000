## Spec


**Overview:** The work as picture book for a young child. Direct seeing, wonder, story form. Used when the realization is intended to plant the question early — that the world might be bigger than the count.

**Voice preset:** Storyteller. See Voice Reference. Override at Register: Child (narrator) or Mythic with Child characters (instead of preset's default Mythic narrator-only) to commit to the child positioning. Affect: Tender (override from preset default Composed) — the narrator addresses the child with care.

**Medium preset:** Watercolor. See Medium Reference.

**Visual World preset:** Storybook. See Visual World Reference.

**Audio preset:** Lecture register. See Audio Aesthetic Reference. Override at Phrasing: include repetition for memory (typical of children's read-aloud) — symmetrical balance with explicit refrains.

**Math density:** Minimal (primitives appear as objects in the story — the stick, the river, the three rods)

**Movement affinities:** Strong I, II, III, V, VII · Workable VI · Resists IV

**Deployment notes:**
- The fairytale and the children's book are close cousins; the difference is the children's book commits more fully to the child positioning, while the fairytale allows mythic narrator framing
- The "girl who walked past the rod" is this realization
- At IV, the child positioning struggles with the cage — typically handled by having a child character ask the question that exposes the cage rather than dwelling inside it
- Avoid drift toward moralizing — preserve the child's directness as seeing, not lesson

**Script preset:** Read-aloud script. See Script Reference.

**Presentation preset:** Picture book. See Presentation Reference.
