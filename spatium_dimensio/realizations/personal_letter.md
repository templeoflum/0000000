# Personal Letter

A realization of *Spatium Dimensio*. Currently a spec without generations — has not been constructed through the workflow.

> **Status:** Unverified. This realization was sketched as part of the framework's original catalog of realization patterns. It exists as a complete cross-layer spec but has not been used to produce tracked generations. When constructed end-to-end through the workflow, its compositional coherence will be verified.

---

## Spec


**Overview:** The work as a letter or voice memo to one specific person. Intimate, direct, the gentle naming of what was felt. Used when the realization is intended for one-to-one address, often in response to someone's specific experience of the bigger-than-three-dimensions feeling.

**Voice preset:** Intimate first-person. See Voice Reference. Override at Register: Caretaker (instead of preset's default Realist) to foreground the addressed-to-someone tenderness. Affect: Tender (override from preset default Composed) — the realization's signature warmth.

**Medium preset:** Watercolor or Ink+wash (handmade, intimate scale). See Medium Reference.

**Visual World preset:** Star-flesh. See Visual World Reference.

**Audio preset:** Quiet voice. See Audio Aesthetic Reference.

**Math density:** Minimal (primitives appear only as gestures toward the felt experience)

**Movement affinities:** Strong I, V, VI, VII · Workable II · Resists III, IV

**Deployment notes:**
- The realization that says "you weren't wrong to feel it bigger"
- At V, the personal letter is at its strongest — the body's wisdom honored, named gently
- Resists III and IV — intimate address can't sustain plurality of traditions or compression of cage
- Cross-axis interaction: caretaker × idealist × intimate is high-vulnerability; deploy when the audience needs it, not when the work needs to be argued
- Avoid drift toward generic comfort-language — preserve the specificity of the addressee's experience

**Script preset:** Voice memo script. See Script Reference.

**Presentation preset:** Intimate page. See Presentation Reference.
