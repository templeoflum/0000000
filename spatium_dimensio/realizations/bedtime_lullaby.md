# Bedtime Lullaby

A realization of *Spatium Dimensio*. Currently a spec without generations — has not been constructed through the workflow.

> **Status:** Unverified. This realization was sketched as part of the framework's original catalog of realization patterns. It exists as a complete cross-layer spec but has not been used to produce tracked generations. When constructed end-to-end through the workflow, its compositional coherence will be verified.

---

## Spec


**Overview:** The work as a sung lullaby cycle — closer to a song-cycle than a children's book. Seven simple folk-melodic songs, sung by a single voice with small acoustic ensemble. The cosmological recognitions rendered at the scale of what you sing a child at bedtime. Used when the realization should reach the body before the mind, and live in oral-musical tradition rather than as text.

**Voice:** Atomic configuration — Musician register × Idealist stance × Intimate positioning × Solo configuration (with Layered configuration option for second-voice harmony at certain movements) × **Tender affect**. Doesn't compose to an existing Voice preset; uses the Musician register (previously underutilized in the catalog) with intimate positioning to get the lullaby-singer's combination of tradition-bearing and one-to-one address. The Tender affect is constitutive — a non-Tender lullaby is a different form (a ballad, a lament, a march); the lullaby's identity depends on softened-by-care address.

**Medium preset:** Watercolor. See Medium Reference. Used for the printed songbook's cover and song-facing illustrations; live performance is unvisual.

**Visual World preset:** Storybook. See Visual World Reference. One illustration per song in the printed songbook. The Storybook preset's warm earth muted palette × ambient illustrative light × expressive loose line × stylized narrative figure × gentle narrative atmosphere reads as folk illustration without needing new sub-function options.

**Audio:** Atomic configuration — Modal tonality × Drone + figure texture × *Acoustic / plucked* timbre + Acoustic / breath-based timbre (voice over plucked accompaniment) × Pulse with drone rhythm × Slow unfolding pacing × Stereo / wide spatialization × *Strophic / verse-refrain* phrasing. Doesn't compose to an existing Audio preset; the Folk modal sub-function combination is the realization's signature — distinct from Sacred drone (sustained, ceremonial) and from Quiet voice (drone-based, unmetered). Uses two of the new Tier 1 options added for this realization.

**Math density:** Minimal. The cosmological content is carried by metaphor — "all the names of the dark" for III's many coordinate systems, "the lantern that was a house" for IV's cathedral. Math is felt, not stated. The Strophic / verse-refrain phrasing means each metaphor lands within a memorable repeated shape.

**Movement affinities:** Strong I, II, VII (intimate, quiet, single-perspective) · Workable VI (gentle recognition fits the lullaby register) · Resists III, IV, V (polyphony, compression, expansion all fight the lullaby's solo intimacy and strophic symmetry)

**Deployment notes:**
- Each movement is one song with a singable melody and lyrics
- Polyphony moments (III, V) handled by either layering a second voice (move Configuration to Layered) or by metaphor (the song *names* the many voices without literally being them)
- IV is the hardest: needs to be a heavier song without becoming oppressive — the cathedral as the "great hall of one rule"
- Drift to avoid: cuteness — the work is gentle but it is not childish; the lullaby tradition has always carried hard truths softly
- The arc closes with VII as a return to the I melody, transformed by everything heard between
- If a second sung realization develops, the audio configuration here (Modal × Drone + figure × Acoustic plucked + breath × Pulse with drone × Slow unfolding × Stereo wide × Strophic) is a candidate to promote to a Tier 2 *Folk song* audio preset
