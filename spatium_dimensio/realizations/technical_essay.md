## Spec


**Overview:** The work as published essay or paper. Direct, structural, math-foregrounded. Used when the realization is intended to make the conceptual content legible to a specialist or general academic audience.

**Voice preset:** Lecturer. See Voice Reference. Affect: Composed (preset default).

**Medium:** Atomic configuration — Screen substrate × Digital shader × Smooth rendered surface × Clean rendered edges × Restricted technical color. No preset bundle matches cleanly; the digital screen substrate isn't currently a Tier 2 medium preset.

**Visual World preset:** Geometric. See Visual World Reference.

**Audio preset:** Silent (for silent essay) or Lecture register (for read-aloud version). See Audio Aesthetic Reference.

**Math density:** High (math is the content)

**Movement affinities:** Strong II, III, IV, V · Workable I, VI, VII · Resists nothing

**Deployment notes:**
- The technical essay is the canonical form for IV — the cage explicitly named with formulas and projection apparatus
- Holds constant across movements better than most realizations; the shift from movement to movement is in content (which primitives are foregrounded), not in voice or surface
- Avoid drift toward popular-science breeziness — preserve mathematician register's structural precision
- Cross-axis friction: skeptic × academic produces hedging; deliberately commit when the spline asks

**Script preset:** Lecture script. See Script Reference.

**Presentation preset:** Academic paper. See Presentation Reference.
